The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads day-first dates month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first. A booking for 7 April comes back as 4 July: day and month swap. It reaches every channel: app, email, and the booking-v2 form, whose hint already says DD/MM/YYYY. This is the bug the queued fix is for.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells people to type the month first. Its date field prints MM/DD/YYYY, so people type the month first, and the form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The swap looks the same as parser, but it comes only through the booking-v3 form and the parser fix does not touch it. The fix is the hint (or the reading) in booking-v3.

shift: the date moves by one day. These reports name the right day and month, a day apart (“Tuesday 12 May, calendar says Monday 11 May”; a client in Lisbon gets Tuesday, a client in Toronto Monday). No day-first reading produces that. It points at time-zone handling when an invite is built. The desk’s table files these under parser with a footnote. They stay counted there, so parser matches the desk’s view, and they count here too, as the problem they show.

How a report is placed

  1. Drop repeats: one id, one report.
  2. The tenant decides, as in causes.ts: a form report from a booking-v3 tenant is hint; any other report from a day-first tenant is parser.
  3. A report the desk read as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) also counts as shift.

Intake’s tag is not used. It is a guess from the reporter’s browser, and the table shows how often it is wrong.

Why one ticket hid three problems

The desk merges on subject alone (decision 0001). People describe all three problems with the same words (“wrong day”, “date is wrong”), so every report landed in #4471 and nobody read the bodies. Decision 0001 accepted that engineering would split tickets later; for #4471 that never happened. It needs a new decision record that replaces it; 0001 itself stays as written.

What is not proven yet

  • hint rests on the tenant’s form, not on the report text: the bodies of hint and parser reports read the same. Confirm in the booking-v3 form code before the fix.
  • shift is a small group of mixed strength. Some show the move plainly; others only say “one day off”, “showed as Monday”, or “the invite arrived before I made the booking”. Treat it as one investigation into time-zone handling; split it if the cause turns out to differ.
